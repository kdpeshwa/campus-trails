What is Campus Trails?

Campus Trails is an early prototype for a student travel community. The idea is to help students discover trips, find travel companions from other colleges, and share trip plans.

Community guidelines

  • Be respectful to every student and organizer.
  • Verify trip organizers and travel operators independently.
  • Share only the personal information needed.
  • Never send money to an unverified person.
  • For minors, involve a parent or guardian and follow college rules.
  • Use qualified guides and follow local safety advisories for treks.
This prototype does not provide identity verification, live group chat, payments, or confirmed bookings yet.
